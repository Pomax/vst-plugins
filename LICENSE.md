Even though I am a software developer by trade, these plugins are not projects I am passionate about, they're just tools I needed. They're effectively Proofs of Concept made using text generators. As such, there are several explicit "zeroes":

- There is zero license on the code. I didn't write it, I don't know where the predictive transformer pulled it from, so it's not copyrighted and has no license that you can agree to.
- There is zero guarantees or warranties. Far as I know it's fine, but you are responsible for deciding whether you trust something like this.
- There is zero promise of maintenance. These are PoC that do what I need, but at the same time they're also all I need. If you think something's useful and you'd like it to do something more, or different, I can certainly give that a think and may even effect those changes, but that's not a promise.
- There is zero commitment to ownership. If there are bugs in the code, I will probably, but only probably, look at fixing them.

As an open source author, this isn't open source. I don't know what to call stuff like this, no one's come up with an adequate term that captures this kind of stuff. I know, I know, you're tempted to say "we call it vibe coding" or "it's called AI slop", which just means you _also_ have no idea what to call it. At its core this is just another version zero that used acceptance test driven development, where how well that practice was followed determines the quality of the result. That doesn't even require knowing who or what wrote the code: the code itself is literally irrelevant in ATDD: if the acceptance criteria and the tests were described well enough, the code produced is effectively irrelevant. That's just plain software engineering: the more you know about how code should be structured, the better you can describe your acceptance criteria and tests. It's absolutely a matter of "experience folks get good results, people who know nothing get slop". It's the script kiddie debate all over again, except now the kids are using GPTs instead of VBA.
