# There is no license

These are vibe coded Proof of Concept plugins, and as such there are several explicit "zeroes":

- There is zero license on the code. I didn't write it, I don't know where the predictive transformer pulled it from, so it's not copyrighted and has no license that you can agree to or rely on.
- There are zero guarantees or warranties. As far as I know it's fine, but you are responsible for deciding whether you trust something like this.
- There is zero promise of maintenance. These are PoC that do what I need, and probably do what a bunch of other folks need, too, but at the same time they're also _all_ I need. If you think of something useful that's missing I can certainly give that a think and may even effect those changes, but that's not a promise.
- There is zero commitment to ownership. If there are bugs in the code, submit an issue with explicits steps to reproduce the problem, and I will try to look at getting them fixed.

As someone with decades of open source experience, this isn't "open source". I don't know what to call stuff like this, no one's come up with an adequate term that captures this kind of stuff.

(I know, I know, you're tempted to say "we call it vibe coding" or "it's called AI slop", which just means you _also_ have no idea what to call it)

At its core, all code in this repo is basically a perpetual version zero that was prototyped using acceptance-test driven development, with the quality of the prototype purely based on how well the acceptance criteria were defined.

This is working code, but it's almost certainly not _good_ code.
