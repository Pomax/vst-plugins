Never use an em dash. Not in chat, not in comments, not in documentation, not
in commit messages, not in test files. Never.

**Why:** the user said so, plainly and more than once.

**How to apply:** use a full stop and a new sentence, a colon, or brackets.
"the frame is made here, and the view is parented into it" instead of "the
frame is made here, and the view is parented into it". Where a range is meant,
use "to". This also covers en dashes in prose. Related: [[plain-language]].
