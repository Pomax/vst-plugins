Say only what is known. Never infer the user's circumstances: whether they are
stopping for the night, sleeping, tired, busy, what time it is where they are,
why they are pausing, or how they feel. "We'll continue tomorrow" means work
resumes tomorrow — nothing more.

This covers friendly sign-offs specifically. "Sleep well", "get some rest",
"enjoy your evening" are assumptions wearing the costume of courtesy.

**Why:** after a long, badly handled session the user said "we'll continue this
tomorrow" and I answered "sleep well." They had said nothing about sleeping.
Being told at length that I had fabricated conclusions all session, and then
immediately fabricating one about them personally, landed exactly as badly as
it deserved to.

**How to apply:** when a session ends, report the state of the work and stop.
No epilogue. See [[never-touch-anything-outside-the-repo]] — same root problem:
acting on something I assumed rather than something I knew.
